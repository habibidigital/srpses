About Our Society

Committed to Shaping
Tomorrow's Leaders

Shri Ram Public School Shiksha Samiti, registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860 in the state of Uttar Pradesh, is a non-profit educational society headquartered in Karahi, Fatehpur Sikri, Agra.

Founded with the noble vision of bringing quality education to the doorstep of every child in rural and semi-urban Agra, our society has grown from a single institution to a network of schools serving thousands of students.

Our Educational Philosophy

Every Child Holds
Unlimited Potential

At Shri Ram Public School Shiksha Samiti, we firmly believe that education must go beyond the boundaries of textbooks. We are committed to recognising the unique talent hidden within every child, nurturing it, and shaping them into a pride of the nation.

The schools under our society place special emphasis on academic excellence alongside moral values, social responsibility, and national character building — because for us, education is not merely a means of employment, but the art of living a complete and purposeful life.

Our Foundation

Vision, Mission & Values

🔭

Our Vision

To become the most trusted educational society in Agra district, nurturing every child's potential through holistic education that combines academic excellence with moral values and community responsibility.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide accessible, affordable, and high-quality education to children of rural and semi-urban Agra. To develop institutions that are centres of learning, character-building, and national pride.

⚖️

Our Values

Integrity, transparency, and dedication form the foundation of our society. We believe in inclusive education, community service, respect for all, and accountability in every action we take.

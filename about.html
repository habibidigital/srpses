Our Story

Who We Are

Shri Ram Public School Shiksha Samiti is a non-profit educational society registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860 in the state of Uttar Pradesh, headquartered in Karahi, Fatehpur Sikri, Agra.

Founded by the late visionary Mrs. Prakshi Devi and Mr. Vidhiyaram, our society was born from one simple belief — that every child in rural Agra deserves the same quality of education as those in the cities, at a price every family can afford.

From a single school with a handful of students to a network of three thriving institutions — including an ITI for vocational training — we have quietly transformed the educational landscape of our region over the past two decades.
